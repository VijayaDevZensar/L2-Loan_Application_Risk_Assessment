## Personal Loan Products

### Standard Personal Loan
- **Purpose**: General personal use (home improvement, education, medical, debt consolidation)
- **Amount range**: £5,000 – £50,000
- **Term**: 12 to 84 months
- **Indicative APR**: 6.9% – 24.9% (rate depends on credit profile and term)
- **Minimum applicant age**: 21
- **Maximum applicant age at loan maturity**: 70

### Premier Personal Loan
- **Purpose**: High-value personal use for Premier customers only
- **Amount range**: £50,001 – £250,000
- **Term**: 24 to 120 months
- **Indicative APR**: 5.5% – 16.9%
- **Eligibility**: Premier banking relationship required; annual income ≥ £75,000

### Home Improvement Loan
- **Purpose**: Exclusively for home renovation or repair works
- **Amount range**: £2,000 – £75,000
- **Term**: 12 to 60 months
- **Indicative APR**: 5.9% – 19.9%
- **Note**: Proof of property ownership may be required

## Eligibility Requirements

All applicants must satisfy ALL of the following:
1. Be a UK resident aged 18 or over
2. Have a regular income (employment, self-employment, or pension)
3. Pass FinTrust's creditworthiness and affordability checks
4. Have no undischarged bankruptcy at the time of application
5. Hold or open a FinTrust current account

## Repayment

- Repayments are made by monthly Direct Debit from your FinTrust current account.
- You may make additional lump-sum payments at any time.
- You may request a repayment holiday of up to 2 months once per loan term (subject to approval and interest accrual).
- Missed payments are reported to credit reference agencies and may attract a late payment fee.

## Application Process

1. Apply online at fintrust.example.com/loans or in branch.
2. FinTrust conducts a soft credit check (no impact on credit score) at pre-qualification.
3. A full credit search is performed on submission of a full application.
4. A trained loan officer reviews the AI-assisted risk triage score before any decision.
5. You will receive a written outcome within 2 business days.
6. If approved, funds are released to your FinTrust current account within 1 business day of signing.
